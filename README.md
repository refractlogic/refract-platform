# REFRACT Platform

Official release packages for the REFRACT Platform by Refract Logic: the platform deployment, its
adapters and its connectors.

The REFRACT Platform generates, signs, delivers and governs documents inside your own Azure
subscription, for Microsoft Dynamics 365, Business Central and Power Platform.

## What is published here

Each release on the [Releases](https://github.com/refractlogic/refract-platform/releases) page contains
the packages for that version, such as:

- **Platform deployment package**: the installer and infrastructure templates for your Azure subscription
- **SharePoint package**: the REFRACT Lifecycle extension for your document libraries
- **Connectors and adapters**: the Power Platform connector and the Dynamics integrations

Release notes for each version describe what changed and anything to do before upgrading.
